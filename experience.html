The classroom, by the numbers

What I teach, and what I've picked up along the way

Eleven years across two schools, five courses, and more curriculum rewrites than I can count. Here's the breakdown.

Current course load

Five courses, one school year

🧬

Biology I — Grade 9

Foundational cell biology, genetics, and evolution. Includes a semester-long garden project where every student tracks a plant from seed to harvest.

3 sectionsCore
🌎

AP Environmental Science

College-level ecology and climate science, built around real watershed data our classes collect from a creek behind the school.

3 sectionsAP
🐚

Marine Ecology Elective

Tide-pool ecosystems, a classroom reef tank, and an annual spring trip to the Oregon coast for field surveys.

1 sectionElective

Intro to Genetics

A short-block elective covering heredity, CRISPR, and bioethics — structured almost entirely around student-led debate.

Career timeline

Where I've taught

2017 – Present

Biology & Environmental Science Teacher — Willow Creek High School

Grew AP Environmental Science from one section to three, launched the Marine Ecology elective, and built the school's first student-run garden and compost program.

2013 – 2017

Science Teacher — Bayview High School

Taught Biology I and Earth Science to grades 9–10; coached the school's first Ocean Sciences Bowl team to a regional finish.

2012 – 2013

Student Teacher & Research Assistant — University of Oregon

Completed a student-teaching placement in a 9th grade science classroom while finishing a tide pool research project.
