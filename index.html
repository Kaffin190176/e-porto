How I teach

Three things I refuse to compromise on

No two classes get the same lesson twice — but every class gets these.

🔬

Hands-on, always

If it can be a lab, a dissection, a build, or a walk outside instead of a worksheet, it will be. Kids remember what they touch.

🌍

Make it matter here

Every unit gets tied to our watershed, our backyard, our news feed. Science that stays in the textbook doesn't stick.

💬

Wrong answers welcome

The fastest way to kill curiosity is to punish a guess. My room runs on questions, not on being right the first time.
