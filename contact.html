Before you write

Quick answers

Do you share lesson plans with other teachers?

Happily — most of what's on the journal is free to adapt. If you want the original files, just ask.

Can you speak at a conference or PD day?

Yes, when the school schedule allows. I speak most often on inquiry-based labs and low-cost field science.

How fast do you reply to parents?

Within two school days, usually faster. For anything urgent, please call the school office directly.

Are you taking student teachers or mentees?

I mentor two first-year teachers a year through the district program — reach out in early spring for next year.
