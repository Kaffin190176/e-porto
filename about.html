The profile

Hi. I'm Elena — I teach kids to love the natural world.

Eleven years, one classroom, roughly six thousand cups of coffee. Here's how I got here, and what I still believe about teaching.

Est. 2013
My story

From marine biology major to the front of a classroom

I didn't set out to teach. I started college convinced I'd spend my life on research boats, counting kelp. A work-study job tutoring in a campus science center changed that — I loved the research, but I loved the moment a concept finally clicked for someone even more.

I finished my B.S. in Marine Biology, picked up a teaching certification, and took my first job at a small coastal high school in 2013. Four years later I moved to Portland to teach at Willow Creek High School, where I've stayed ever since — through three different classrooms, one pandemic, and more compost bin redesigns than I'd like to admit.

These days I teach Biology I, AP Environmental Science, a Marine Ecology elective, and a short Genetics unit. Outside of Room 214 I co-lead the school's sustainability committee, referee too many staff trivia nights, and am, very slowly, restoring an old canoe.

B.S. Marine Biology M.Ed. Curriculum & Instruction National Board Certified
Teaching philosophy

"My job isn't to hand kids answers. It's to make them annoyed enough by a question that they go find the answer themselves — and then come tell me about it."

The path here

A (mostly) chronological career

2009 – 2013

B.S. in Marine Biology, University of Oregon

Spent three summers as a research assistant surveying tide pools along the Oregon coast — and realized I liked explaining the data more than collecting it.

2013 – 2017

Science Teacher, Bayview High School

First classroom, first 6am bus duty, first time watching a kid choose a science fair project because they actually wanted to know the answer.

2016

M.Ed., Curriculum & Instruction, Portland State University

Completed my master's part-time while teaching, focused on inquiry-based science curriculum design.

2017 – present

Biology & Environmental Science Teacher, Willow Creek High School

Built the AP Environmental Science program from a single section into three, launched the Marine Ecology elective, and started the school's first student-run garden.

2021

National Board Certification, Adolescence & Young Adulthood Science

Two years of portfolio work, video review, and rewriting the same reflection five times — worth every hour.

2023

District Teacher of the Year

Nominated by students and colleagues for the sustainability curriculum redesign and mentoring first-year science teachers.
